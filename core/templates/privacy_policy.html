Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

This notice describes information used by the current GLINT demonstration application. It is informational and should be reviewed and adapted before collecting real customer data in production.

Information used by the site

When you use relevant features, GLINT may store account details such as your name and email; profile and delivery details such as phone number and address; saved gift-box drafts; and order, payment-method selection, tracking-reference, and personalization details.

How information is used

The application uses this information to operate accounts, save customizations, place and display orders, manage fulfillment, and send password-reset, order-confirmation, or order-status messages when email is configured.

Storage and access

Information is stored in the database configured for this deployment. Passwords are handled by Django’s authentication system. Staff users can access order and catalog information through the admin portal. Session and security-related cookies support sign-in and form protection.

Payments and third parties

Online checkout is in test mode and does not collect real payment credentials or contact a payment provider. No online payment is charged. Cash on Delivery orders are payable upon delivery. Email delivery depends on the deployment configuration; development may write email messages to the server console. Hosting, database, and email infrastructure may be provided by the operator’s configured services.

Questions

For questions about information handled by this demonstration, contact support@glintgifts.com.
